## Key Considerations

- **PSU compatibility:** Flex ATX is most common. Some cases support Mean Well LOP-300, ATX, or SFX. Check before printing.
- **Heatsink mod required:** Most cases assume you have opened the fin stack on the stock heatsink. See [Cooling Guide](04-cooling-guide.md).
- **Nylon washers:** 4x M3 nylon washers under heatsink screws prevent shorting. See [Cooling Guide > CRITICAL](04-cooling-guide.md).
- **Filament:** PETG or PETG-CF recommended for heat resistance. PLA may deform near the VRM/backplate area.
- **Threaded inserts:** Many designs require M3 heat-set inserts.
- **WIP designs:** Some threads are early concepts. Verify availability before starting a print.
